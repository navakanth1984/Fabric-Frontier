# DEAD LOOP: Character & Environment Bible

This document serves as the absolute visual and physical source of truth for the **Dead Loop** production pipeline. Every image generation prompt (FVP-Image) and motion generation brief (FVP-Video) must reference this document to maintain visual consistency across all sequences and prevent character/scene drift.

---

## 🧑 CHARACTER BIBLE

### 1. Arjun (The Protagonist)
*   **Role:** Cybernetic renegade struggling to keep his humanity.
*   **Physical Blueprint:**
    -   **Age/Heritage:** 28-year-old South Asian male.
    -   **Face:** Sharp jawline, defined V-line cheekbones, short textured hair with a clean side fade, thin weathered stubble along his jaw.
    -   **Eyes:** Intense dark brown eyes that reflect a deep mixture of raw exhaustion, grief, and sharp, calculating focus.
    -   **Skin:** Dewy porcelain-like texture, covered in faint sweat and rain glaze, realistic pore fidelity.
*   **Cybernetic Port Details:**
    -   **Locations:** Exposed neural interface ports snaking along the upper spine and base of the neck.
    -   **Aesthetic:** Fused carbon-alloy ports with a translucent shell revealing intricate, glowing fiber-optic veins inside.
    -   **Indicators:** Glows with a slow, rhythmic red light that pulses in a three-pulse heartbeat pattern. Arjun's left eye features a tiny cybernetic iris ring that occasionally flickers with a cool cyan data display.
*   **Production Attire:**
    -   **Primary:** Long, weathered charcoal-duster duster coat with high-tech matte-black shoulder pads.
    -   **Secondary:** Fitted matte-black tactical jacket with a high collar, paired with a dark techwear utility chest strap and weathered silver hardware.
    -   **Materials:** Matte leather, wet heavy canvas, and exposed alloy joints.

### 🤖 VEDA (The AI Companion)
*   **Role:** Sentient, learning artificial intelligence manifested through local systems.
*   **Aesthetic Core:** Fluid light, particle physics, and corrupted code streams.
*   **Color DNA:** Cool cyan (#00FFFF) and terminal green (#39FF14) as the stable state; jaundiced yellow (#FFFF33) and vermillion/orange (#FF4500) during glitching states.
*   **Visual Motifs:**
    -   **Stable State:** Volumetric data streams, floating geometric code rings, clean Sanskrit vertical glyph scrolling.
    -   **Glitch State:** Three-pulse heartbeat fluctuations, blocky digital noise, screen scanlines, horizontal video tearing.
    -   **Manifestation:** Appears holographically as an ethereal, faceless outline composed of hundreds of glowing cyan light particles.

## 🌆 ENVIRONMENT BIBLE

### 🌧️ 1. Old City Hyderabad (Analog Decay)
*   **Visual Tone:** Indian Cyberpunk Noir, dense, wet, crumbling.
*   **Architecture:** Labyrinthine brick alleys, ancient arched doorways, wet asphalt roads, and an oppressive, tangled canopy of black overhead power and data cables.
*   **Lighting:** High-contrast chiaroscuro. Low-saturation warm amber and vermillion streetlights reflecting on rain-slicked brick walls and deep water puddles. Thick, heavy atmospheric blue night mist.

### 🏙️ 2. HITECCity (Sterile Digital Monolith)
*   **Visual Tone:** Sterile, high-tech corporate control, vast, sleek.
*   **Architecture:** Towering monolithic skyscrapers clad in clean glass panels, towering concrete bridges, and skybridges dangling with glowing high-voltage nodes.
*   **Lighting:** Cold blue, cyan, and violet ambient glow from massive corporate buildings. Harsh, geometric searchlights of steel-blue casting thick volumetric beams through the smog. Large, holographic advertisements displaying corporate propaganda ("EFFICIENCY IS FREEDOM").

### 🔱 3. The VedaCore Room (The Submerged Server Vault)
*   **Visual Tone:** Post-apocalyptic high-tech cathedral, decaying, eerie.
*   **Architecture:** A massive brutalist concrete server vault completely flooded with a thin layer of stagnant rainwater. Towers of rusted, decaying server racks rise from the water.
*   **Lighting:** Spectral green-blue glow emanating from submerged emergency panels and jaundiced yellow from leaking bioluminescent cooling vents. Deep, heavy volumetric shadows with misty ceiling skylights filtering cold daylight.
