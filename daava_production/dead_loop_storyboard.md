# DEAD LOOP: Cinematic Trailer Storyboard

**Aesthetic Core:** Indian Cyberpunk Noir, Hyderabad. Amber vs. Neon Green. Wet concrete, CRT phosphor glow, chiaroscuro.

## Sequence 1: The Descent (0-15s)
- **Shot 1.1 (0-5s):** Extreme wide, high-angle drone shot diving into the narrow, rain-slicked alleys of Old City Hyderabad. Neon signs reflect in the puddles.
- **Shot 1.2 (5-10s):** Medium tracking shot of Arjun walking through the crowded, neon-lit bazaar. He wears a weathered, tactical duster coat. The camera pushes in slightly.
- **Shot 1.3 (10-15s):** Low-angle close-up. Arjun stops at a neon-green puddle. The reflection shows not just his face, but a flickering cybernetic overlay.

## Sequence 2: The Interface (15-30s)
- **Shot 2.1 (15-20s):** Extreme close-up on a vintage CRT monitor powering on. Amber phosphor text scrolls rapidly: "INITIATING DEAD_LOOP".
- **Shot 2.2 (20-25s):** Over-the-shoulder shot. Arjun interfaces with a bulky, retro-futuristic terminal. Wires connect directly to a port on his neck.
- **Shot 2.3 (25-30s):** Fast Dolly-in. The terminal screen erupts in bright neon green, illuminating Arjun's face in stark high-key lighting.

## Sequence 3: The Chase (30-45s)
- **Shot 3.1 (30-35s):** Action wide shot. Arjun sprints across a rooftop, leaping over a gap. The HITECCity skyline looms in the background, contrasting sleek glass with his gritty surroundings.
- **Shot 3.2 (35-40s):** Tracking close-up on his boots splashing through deep water on the roof, sending neon-lit droplets flying in slow motion.
- **Shot 3.3 (40-45s):** Dutch angle, medium shot. Arjun slides behind an aged copper ventilation unit as searchlights sweep the area.

## Sequence 4: The Revelation (45-60s)
- **Shot 4.1 (45-52s):** Slow pan across a massive, abandoned server farm submerged in shallow water. Cables hang like vines.
- **Shot 4.2 (52-57s):** Close-up on Arjun's face, bathed in a soft, ethereal amber light. He looks up in awe.
- **Shot 4.3 (57-60s):** The camera pulls back rapidly to reveal a giant, holographic mandala floating above the servers. Smash to black. Title card: DEAD LOOP.
