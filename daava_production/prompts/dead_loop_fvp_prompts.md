# FVP Prompt Package: DEAD LOOP Complete Storyboard

This asset package contains the complete, production-ready **Flow Visual Pipeline (FVP)** prompts for all 12 shots described in the **Dead Loop Cinematic Storyboard**.

Each shot includes:
1.  **FVP-Image Prompt:** Optimized for **Google Flow / Imagen** to generate high-fidelity, safety-compliant starting frames.
2.  **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):** Engineered to guide physics-based motion, fluid/particle dynamics, and complex cinematic camera moves.

---

## 🎬 Production Naming Standards
*   **Sequence 1:** The Descent (Old City)
*   **Sequence 2:** The Interface (Terminal room)
*   **Sequence 3:** The Chase (Rooftop skyline)
*   **Sequence 4:** The Revelation (Submerged server vault)

## 🌧️ SEQUENCE 1: THE DESCENT

### Shot 1.1: The Alley Descent
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC01_SH01`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > An extreme wide high-angle aerial drone shot diving into the narrow, labyrinthine, rain-slicked brick alleys of Old City Hyderabad at night. Glow of warm amber, terminal green, and vermillion neon street signs reflect beautifully in wet asphalt and deep puddles. Ancient wooden doors, crumbling archways, and overhead tangle of black power cables. Bathed in atmospheric night mist. Shot on Arri Alexa 65, 18mm lens, deep depth of field, high-contrast chiaroscuro, cinematic cyberpunk noir aesthetic.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: The camera performs a fast, dynamic vertical diving drone motion down into the narrow brick alley. Soft rain falls in slow motion, creating realistic splash ripples in the water puddles. Neon lights flicker subtly through the rising atmospheric mist.

### Shot 1.2: The Bazaar Tracking
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC01_SH02`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A medium eye-level tracking shot of a 28-year-old South Asian male, Arjun, walking forward through a crowded, dense, neon-lit Hyderabad bazaar at night. He wears a long, weathered charcoal duster duster coat with high-tech matte-black shoulder pads and cybernetic interface port outlines. Background features blurred market stalls, glowing terminal screens, and modern neon street banners. Lit by a cool cyan side-light and a warm amber backlight. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 50mm f/1.4 lens, shallow depth of field, realistic clothing folds, cinematic cyberpunk atmosphere.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: Camera performs a steady, slow forward push-in tracking Arjun's movement. Arjun walks smoothly with a slight head tilt, looking alertly at his surroundings. The ambient crowd in the background drifts slowly, and rain droplets fall realistically onto the shoulders of his tactical coat.

### Shot 1.3: The Neon Reflection
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC01_SH03`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A low-angle close-up shot of Arjun looking down at a large neon-green puddle on a rain-slicked concrete floor. The water surface acts as a mirror, showing the reflection of his face—28-year-old South Asian male with short textured hair and stubble—but the reflection is digitally overlaid with a glowing cyan cybernetic circuit pattern and flickering terminal code lines. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 35mm lens, high contrast, sharp details on both his boots in the foreground and the water's surface, cinematic noir aesthetic.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: The camera slowly tilts down toward the puddle. The water's surface ripples dynamically from falling raindrops, creating a fluid wave distortion across Arjun's reflection. The cybernetic neon-green and cyan overlay in the reflection glitches, sparks, and scroll-cycles code lines.

## 🖥️ SEQUENCE 2: THE INTERFACE

### Shot 2.1: The Flicker of Obsolete Code
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC02_SH01`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > An extreme close up shot of an archaic, curved glass CRT monitor screen displaying lines of warm amber phosphor code in a clean terminal layout against a deep black glass void. The curved CRT screen features subtle scanlines and a glossy reflective surface showing faint, blurred highlights of an industrial concrete room. High-contrast, dark-mode cyberpunk noir aesthetic. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 50mm f/2.0 lens, shallow depth of field, sharp amber text glow with soft halo bloom, moody analog-digital contrast.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: The camera executes a slow, highly deliberate mechanical push-in toward the screen until the curved CRT frame fills the frame. The amber phosphor text flickers to life suddenly, buzzing with analog voltage fluctuations. Faint digital noise and slight static lines ripple vertically across the amber phosphor letters.

### Shot 2.2: The Cybernetic Interface
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC02_SH02`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A tight over-the-shoulder shot of a 28-year-old South Asian male character, Arjun, sitting in front of a retrofitted vintage terminal. Thick, textured black cybernetic wires snake out of neural interface ports on the back of his neck, directly connecting into the metal chassis of the terminal. His hands are poised over a physical steel keyboard. Bathed in a sickly amber and warm glow. Background shows a dark room with exposed wiring. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 35mm lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field, sharp detail on the coiled neck wires and neck skin texture, realistic clothing material.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: Camera dollies forward in a slow, relentless push over Arjun's shoulder. His fingers move dynamically across the keyboard. A subtle, low-frequency red light pulses through the translucent portions of the cybernetic neck ports, syncing with a three-pulse heartbeat. Subtle mist rises from the terminal's ventilation vents.

### Shot 2.3: The Eruption of Green Light
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC02_SH03`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A close-up front-facing shot of Arjun, a 28-year-old South Asian man with sweat-slicked skin, his face fully illuminated by a sick, phosphorescent neon green light erupting from an off-camera CRT monitor. Deep, dramatic shadows carved under his cheekbones and brow, his dark brown irises reflecting bright green pixelated code. High-contrast, moody cyberpunk lighting. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 85mm f/1.4 lens, razor-sharp focus on the glinting reflection in his eyes, soft background bokeh.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: The camera starts in a medium shot and executes an accelerating dolly-in straight toward Arjun's face. The neon green light casting across his features pulses rapidly like a hyperactive heartbeat, while glitching holographic data streams run across his eyes' reflections. Subtle rapid eye blinks and slight twitching of the jaw muscles to emphasize intense panic and concentration.

## 🏃 SEQUENCE 3: THE CHASE

### Shot 3.1: The Rooftop Sprint
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC03_SH01`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > An extreme widescreen shot of a grimy South Asian male in a tactical black jacket sprinting across a dark, wet rooftop. A gaping chasm of a collapsed skybridge lies ahead, with broken cables and dangling metal parts. The background features the massive, gleaming modern HITECCity skyline at night, with towering glass structures glowing in icy cyan, violet, and silver. A flickering orange and green neon sign is positioned on the left. Shot on Arri Alexa 65, 24mm anamorphic lens, high contrast, wet surface reflections, dramatic depth of field.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: The camera tracks Arjun's sprint at a low angle, then ascends vertically as he leaps across the gap, revealing the sheer height of the cityscape beneath him. Heavy rain falls in slow motion, forming realistic water splashes under his feet. Frayed cables dangle and swing dynamically in the wind. The landing ends with a sudden, disorienting handheld camera whip-pan.

### Shot 3.2: The Rain-Flooded Liminal Space
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC03_SH02`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A wide cinematic shot of a flat, concrete rooftop completely flooded with a thin layer of stagnant rainwater, acting as a perfect mirror reflecting the towering, corporate skyscrapers of HITECCity at night. The sky is dark, heavy with blue smog and gentle rainfall. A single silhouette of a man stands in the middle-distance. The color palette is dominated by cool slate blues, cyan reflections, and sharp white corporate logos. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 28mm lens, sharp reflections across the entire flooded surface, atmospheric soft lighting.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: Camera slides laterally in a slow, smooth tracking motion. Raindrops fall continuously, creating realistic concentric ripple patterns that propagate across the water mirror, distorting the reflected skyscrapers. The dark blue smog and ambient neon lights drift slowly in the background wind.

### Shot 3.3: Behind the Vent
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC03_SH03`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A close-up shot framed at a dramatic 45-degree Dutch angle. Arjun, panting with raw exhaustion, is hiding behind a rusted, industrial steel ventilation unit. Harsh, geometric searchlights of icy steel-blue and cyan slice through the dark, misty air from off-screen, creating high-contrast volumetric beams. His tactical black jacket is wet with rain. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 50mm f/1.4 lens, razor-sharp focus on the fear in his eyes, high-contrast cyberpunk noir composition.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: The camera maintains the disorienting Dutch angle while slowly dollying inward. Volumetric searchlights sweep dynamically across the ventilation unit and Arjun's face, casting shifting shadows. Rain falls heavily through the searchlight beams, with realistic droplets streaking down the wet metal and Arjun's forehead.

## 🔱 SEQUENCE 4: THE REVELATION

### Shot 4.1: The Submerged Server Vault
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC04_SH01`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A wide wide-angle shot of a massive, flooded brutalist server vault. Racks of rusted, decaying server towers rise from the dark water, partially submerged. Sickly green and toxic-blue emergency panels glow beneath the water's surface, casting jagged neon reflections. Moss-covered data cables hang like vines from the ceiling, sparking weakly. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 21mm lens, deep volumetric shadows, eerie reflection on the calm water, post-apocalyptic cyberpunk aesthetic.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: Camera executes a slow, smooth dolly pan from left to right, skimming inches above the water's surface. Submerged light panels flicker erratically, sending ripples of blue-green light through the water. Heavy mossy cables sway gently, dripping water droplets that splash into the flooded floor.

### Shot 4.2: The Reflection of Amber
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC04_SH02`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A close-up profile shot of Arjun, his face completely illuminated by a soft, warm amber glow materializing in front of him. His eyes are wide with awe, his irises reflecting a delicate, glowing orange code structure like fractured glass. The background is a cold, dark void of slate-blue and indigo. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 85mm f/1.4 lens, shallow depth of field, sharp facial details, skin pore resolution, warm amber rim lighting.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: Camera performs a slow, hypnotic dolly-in toward his eyes. The reflected amber code in his irises moves and scrolls slowly. His cybernetic eye implant faintly flickers with a cyan indicator light, and tiny circuit patterns pulse beneath his skin in synchronization with the amber data streams.

### Shot 4.3: The Holographic Mandala Crash
*   **Asset Code:** `DL_SC04_SH03`
*   **FVP-Image Prompt (Google Flow):**
    > A massive widescreen shot of a cavernous server room. In the center, a gigantic, complex holographic mandala pulses with cobalt blue, fractured purple, and jaundiced yellow light. Glitching Sanskrit glyphs orbit the perimeter of the mandala. The surrounding server racks are a fusion of machinery and organic tissue, with vents releasing thick steam. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 24mm lens, high-contrast, epic scale, hyper-detailed volumetric hologram.
*   **FVP-Video Prompt (Flow Omni):**
    > animate this reference image: The camera executes an intense dolly zoom (zolly) effect, whipping backward rapidly to compress perspective as the scale of the Sanskrit mandala expands. The mandala's geometric rings spin in opposite directions, emitting glowing particles that drift through the thick steam. The shot ends with a sudden, violent flicker before cutting instantly to pitch black.
