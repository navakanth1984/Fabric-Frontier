INT. INTERROGATION ROOM - NIGHT

The sound of the monsoon storm rages outside, but the small, windowless room is dimly lit and eerily quiet. Water seeps through the cracks in the walls, forming a small puddle on the floor. Arjun paces back and forth, his eyes fixed on Suresh Yadav.

SURESH YADAV
(sitting calmly, peeling a mango)

Arjun stops pacing and stares at Yadav, his anger simmering just below the surface.

ARJUN
(through gritted teeth)
What do you want from me?

SURESH YADAV
(taking a bite of the mango, savoring the sweetness)
I want to know the truth.

ARJUN
(sneering)
You'll never get it from me.

SURESH YADAV
(leaning forward, his eyes glinting in the dim light)
We'll see about that.

Yadav takes another bite of the mango, the juice dripping down his chin. Arjun's eyes flash with rage, and he takes a step forward, his fists clenched.

ARJUN
(seething)
You think you're clever, don't you? Eating a mango while I'm stuck in this pit.

SURESH YADAV
(chuckling)
It's a good mango.

Arjun's anger boils over, and he lunges at Yadav, but the older man is too quick. He dodges out of the way just in time, and Arjun crashes into the wall, his fists pounding against the concrete.

ARJUN
(enraged)
You'll pay for this!

SURESH YADAV
(still eating the mango)
I'm shaking in my boots.

The storm outside rages on, but in the small room, the tension is even more intense. Arjun's rage is building, and it's only a matter of time before it consumes him completely.

FADE OUT:
