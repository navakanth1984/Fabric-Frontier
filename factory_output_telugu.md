**INT. SURESH YADAV'S OFFICE - DAY**

SURESH YADAV, a middle-aged man with a commanding presence, sits behind his desk, sipping a cup of tea. ARJUN, a young man in his late 20s, stands opposite him, his eyes fixed on the floor.

ARJUN: (mumbling) Cheptuna, Suresh garu...

SURESH YADAV: (looking up, a hint of amusement in his voice) Eh, Arjun? Oka matladakundam? (What's on your mind, Arjun?)

ARJUN: (hesitating) Water supply lo problems vunnayi, Suresh garu. People complaining vunnaru... (There are problems with the water supply, Suresh sir. People are complaining...)

SURESH YADAV: (chuckling) Water supply? Ah, vaalla vaadu, intha paani ki pani koddinappudu... (Water supply? Ha, those people, they'll drink whatever water they get...)

Arjun's eyes flicker, a spark of defiance igniting within him.

ARJUN: (taking a step forward) Vaallu nannu ayyi dikkuda? Veellu paani thagga rasaru, anna... (Are they afraid of me? They're scared of you, brother...)

Suresh Yadav's expression changes, his eyes narrowing.

SURESH YADAV: (leaning forward, his voice low and menacing) Nee dikkuda, Arjun? Nee vaalla nannu? (Are you afraid of me, Arjun? You think you're not afraid of me?)

Arjun takes another step forward, his voice steady.

ARJUN: (looking Suresh Yadav straight in the eye) Nee vaadina sainyaaniki nannu dikkuda, Suresh garu. Veellu nannu ayyi dikkuda... (I'm afraid of your goons, Suresh sir. But they're the ones who are afraid of me...)

Suresh Yadav's smile returns, but this time it's tinged with unease.

SURESH YADAV: (leaning back in his chair) Vaalla paani ki pani koddinappudu, Arjun. Veellu ninnu ayyi dikkuda... (Those people will drink whatever water they get, Arjun. They're the ones who are afraid of you...)

Arjun's eyes never leave Suresh Yadav's face.

ARJUN: (his voice dripping with malice) Nannu ayyi dikkuda, Suresh garu... (You're the one who's afraid of me, Suresh sir...)

The air in the room becomes charged with tension as Arjun's words hang in the air, a challenge to Suresh Yadav's authority.

**FADE TO BLACK**
