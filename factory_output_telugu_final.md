INT. INTERROGATION ROOM - NIGHT

The monsoon rain DRUMS on the tin roof—a sound like lead pellets. A single bulb, yellow and caked with dust, sways above a metal table.

SURESH YADAV (50s) leans back. He is a man of weight and silence. He holds a massive Banganapalli mango. He doesn't use a knife. He TEARS the skin with his teeth. 

Juice splatters his white silk kurta. He doesn't care. He owns the kurta. He owns the room. He owns the air.

ARJUN (34) sits opposite him. He is motionless. His soaked shirt clings to his ribs. He doesn't pace. He doesn't blink. He is a statue of salt.

YADAV
(Mouth full, juice dripping)
Nampally lo andaru ninnu 'Saab' antunnaru anta... Endi ra ee hadavidi? 

Arjun says nothing. His silence is a physical wall. 

YADAV
(Chuckles, teeth yellow with pulp)
Gaali kooda nenu chepthe ne aagutadi ikkada. Nuuvu vachi 'reliability' antava? 'Justice' antava? 

Yadav SLAPS the mango pit onto the table. It slides through the juice, leaving a sticky trail. 

YADAV
Cheppu ra. Em kaavali? Dabbu kaavala? Leka mee amma bed daggara nenu kurchuni 'sorry' cheppala?

ARJUN
(Low, a whisper)
Nuvvu bed daggara unnau, Suresh.

Yadav stops chewing. A beat.

ARJUN
Ward 4. July 12th. Midnight. 

Yadav leans forward, trying to maintain the 'messy' dominance. He wipes a hand across his mouth, leaving a smear of pulp.

YADAV
Endi ra gola? July ante monsoon... maintenance untadi...

ARJUN
(Voice cutting like a scalpel)
Maintenance kaadu. Nuvvu oxygen supplier ki call cheshnav. 'Payment late aitadi, pressure thagginchu' ani. Endukante vadu nee construction company ki contractor. Logic simple undi. 

Arjun leans into the light. For the first time, he is the one invading Yadav's space.

ARJUN
Vadu oxygen thagginchindu. Nuuvu oxygen lines nunchi kooda 10 percent commission kottinavu. Motham 42 lakhs. 

Yadav reaches for his silk handkerchief. His hand trembles—just a micro-vibration.

ARJUN
Adhey ledger la, July 14th roju... nuvvu oka plot konnavu. Banjara Hills la. 

Arjun taps the sticky mango juice on the table with his index finger. He looks Yadav in the eye.

ARJUN
Mee kotha intlo oxygen bagundi anukunta, Suresh. 

Yadav stares at Arjun. The "Predator" mask is cracking. He looks at his own sticky hands—suddenly they look like they are covered in blood, not juice.

ARJUN
(Standing up slowly)
Ee saari 'Daava' paani gurinchi kaadu. Nee pranam gurinchi. 

Arjun doesn't look back. He walks to the door.

YADAV
(To himself, barely audible)
Evadu ra veedu... 

Arjun stops at the door. He doesn't turn.

ARJUN
Nenu nee accounting error ni, Suresh. Correct cheyaniki vachna.

FADE OUT.
