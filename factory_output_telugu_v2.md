INT. INTERROGATION ROOM - NIGHT

Arjun, a young man with a determined look, sits across Suresh Yadav, a middle-aged MLA with a sly grin. Yadav's hands are sticky with mango juice as he peels the fruit.

ARJUN:
Nuvvu claim chestunnava, nijam ante edo undi?

SURESH YADAV:
(looking at Arjun with a mixture of amusement and curiosity)
Em cheptunav, abbai? Nuvvu elanti claims cheste vaddu?

ARJUN:
(gripping his fists)
Nuvvu elanti vishwasanni kattav, motham vishwasanni kattav. Kani nijam ante edo undi?

SURESH YADAV:
(taking a bite of the mango)
Ah, nijam ante... (chuckles) Nuvvu vishwasanni kattav, vishwasanni kattav. Kani nijam ante... (shrugs)

ARJUN:
(leaning forward, his voice rising)
Nuvvu elanti vishwasanni kattav, motham vishwasanni kattav. Kani nijam ante edo undi? Gadide vishwasanni kattav, katam vishwasanni kattav.

SURESH YADAV:
(still eating the mango, his eyes locked on Arjun)
Endira, abbai... nijam ante... (pauses, looks at Arjun with a sly grin) Nuvvu elanti vishwasanni kattav, vishwasanni kattav. Kani nijam ante... (shrugs again)

ARJUN:
(slams his fist on the table, making Yadav jump)
Paaye, ayya! Nuvvu aithe vishwasanni kattav, motham vishwasanni kattav. Kani nijam ante edo undi?

Yadav's grin falters, and he looks at Arjun with a hint of surprise. He slowly puts the mango down, his sticky hands hovering over the fruit.

SURESH YADAV:
(looking at Arjun with a mixture of curiosity and caution)
Emayindi, abbai? Nuvvu elanti vishwasanni kattav?

ARJUN:
(leaning back, his eyes locked on Yadav)
Nuvvu elanti vishwasanni kattav, motham vishwasanni kattav. Kani nijam ante edo undi?

Yadav's eyes narrow, and he slowly wipes his hands on his shirt. The mango lies untouched on the table.

FADE OUT.
