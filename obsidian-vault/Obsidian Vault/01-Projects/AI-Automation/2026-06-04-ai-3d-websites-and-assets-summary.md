# AI 3D Websites and Assets Summary

To proceed with AI-driven 3D website development and integration, you can follow a structured workflow that divides the process into generating 3D assets, preparing them for animation, and integrating them into a web environment using specialized AI website builders. 

Here is a detailed summary of how to execute this process:

### 1. Generating 3D Assets with AI
The first step is to create the 3D models you want to feature on your website. There are several AI tools tailored for this depending on your needs:

*   **Meshy AI:** This tool allows you to generate 3D objects using "Text to 3D" or "Image to 3D" prompts. For example, you can upload a 2D JPEG of a product, and the AI will generate four 3D variants in under a minute. You can define the target polygon count (e.g., 10,000 for smooth edges) and choose whether to apply AI texturing or leave it as a white clay model. 
*   **Spline AI:** Operating directly within an editor or dashboard, Spline AI generates 3D models from text prompts (e.g., "a stylized pink sofa") and 2D front-facing images. It also allows you to easily remix and generate new variants based on your previous results.
*   **Alternative Developer Tools:** Game developers and 3D hobbyists also recommend **Hyper3D Rodin** for producing models with clean, sharp geometry and good topology, **Hunyuan3D** for generous free daily generations, and **Tripo AI**, which excels at converting 2D images of 3D structures and features studio rigging capabilities.

### 2. Animating and Preparing the Assets
If your website requires animated characters (like a mascot), AI can automate the complex rigging process:
*   Using **Meshy AI**, you can click the "animate" and "rig" functions to automatically build a skeleton for your character. The AI handles the rigging without requiring manual technical skills, resulting in a smoothly animated model.
*   Once your asset is generated and animated, you must export it in a web-compatible format. Standard formats available for download include **glTF (GLB), FBX, and USDZ**, which can carry your model, textures, and animation data to your web builder.

### 3. Integrating Assets into 3D Websites
To integrate your models into a functional webpage without complex traditional coding, you can use AI-powered web environments:

**Option A: Dora AI (For highly customized visual editing)**
*   Dora uses AI to generate fully interactive 3D websites from a simple text requirement, like "website for a trendy brand," instantly laying out environments, styling, and images.
*   You can directly import the custom glTF 3D models you created in Meshy into Dora's workspace.
*   Dora features an intuitive keyframe system that lets you tie 3D animations to the user's scroll behavior. By setting a "scroll offset," you can make your 3D imported object (like a phone or a key) rotate seamlessly on its axis as the visitor scrolls down the page. 
*   Alternatively, Dora allows you to create 3D effects using pre-rendered image sequences (images of an object at different angles) rather than rendering a full 3D object in the browser.

**Option B: Lovable (For AI-generated website code)**
*   Lovable acts as an AI programmer that actually codes out websites based on your prompts. 
*   You can instruct Lovable to build complex landing pages with specific interactive 3D requirements, such as prompting it to create "a landing page for a company with a 3D shoe that rotates while we pull the page and stays centered." The AI will iteratively code a clean, well-spaced site with the functional 3D scrolling mechanics built in.

**Option C: Spline AI Web Export (For quick embedding)**
*   If you choose to generate your assets entirely within Spline AI, the platform offers native multi-platform publishing. You can directly embed your interactive 3D content into any standard web page using an auto-generated code snippet or custom code.
